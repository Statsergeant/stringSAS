# stringSAS 0.1.0

* Initial release of `stringSAS`.
* Added six SAS-inspired character manipulation functions:
  `sas_compress()`, `sas_scan()`, `sas_substr()`, `sas_catx()`,
  `sas_find()`, and `sas_tranwrd()`.
* Added support for vectorized character operations in R.
* Added documentation, examples, and unit tests.
